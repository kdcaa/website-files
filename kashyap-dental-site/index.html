Frequently asked questions

How often should I get a dental check-up?

For most people, about every six months. Some need visits more or less often depending on their gums, cavities and general health; your dentist will advise.

Do you treat children?

Yes. We see children from their first tooth onwards, with gentle first visits, fluoride and sealants.

Is root canal treatment painful?

It's done under local anaesthesia, so most people feel little during the procedure; many say it feels much like getting a filling. Some tenderness for a few days afterwards is normal.

Do you offer braces and clear aligners in Butwal?

Yes. Every braces and clear aligner case is planned and treated by Dr. Madhav Pandey, MDS orthodontist, so you don't need to travel to Kathmandu.

How do I book?

Use the booking form, message us on WhatsApp, or call +977 974-3679953. We'll confirm a time with you.

Is Kashyap Dental & Aesthetics the same as Kashyap Dental Clinic in India?

No. We are not connected to any clinic in India. Kashyap Dental & Aesthetics is a Nepali clinic in Butwal, on Tilottama Path (Hospital Line), next to Gastrocare.

Until you see us

  1. Rinse gently with warm salt water, and floss to remove any trapped food.
  2. Hold a cold compress on the outside of the cheek, 15 minutes at a time, if there is swelling.
  3. Take an over-the-counter painkiller only as directed on the pack, if it is safe for you.
  4. Don’t place a tablet or aspirin on the gum; it can burn the gum.

Go to a hospital emergency now if

  • swelling is spreading to the eye, jaw or neck
  • you have trouble swallowing or breathing
  • you have a high fever with the pain
  • you’ve had an injury to the face or jaw

Ask a question
How often should I get a dental check-up?

For most people, about every six months. Some need visits more or less often depending on their gums, cavities and general health; your dentist will advise.

Do you treat children?

Yes. We see children from their first tooth onwards, with gentle first visits, fluoride and sealants.

Is root canal treatment painful?

It's done under local anaesthesia, so most people feel little during the procedure; many say it feels much like getting a filling. Some tenderness for a few days afterwards is normal.

Do you offer braces and clear aligners in Butwal?

Yes. Every braces and clear aligner case is planned and treated by Dr. Madhav Pandey, MDS orthodontist, so you don't need to travel to Kathmandu.

How do I book?

Use the booking form, message us on WhatsApp, or call +977 974-3679953. We'll confirm a time with you.

Is Kashyap Dental & Aesthetics the same as Kashyap Dental Clinic in India?

No. We are not connected to any clinic in India. Kashyap Dental & Aesthetics is a Nepali clinic in Butwal, on Tilottama Path (Hospital Line), next to Gastrocare.

When should I start a bridal smile plan?

As early as you can. If teeth need straightening, six months to a year before the wedding is ideal; whitening and polishing are done in the final weeks.

When should my child first see a dentist?

When the first tooth comes in, or by the first birthday. Early visits catch problems early and help children get used to the dentist.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Any previous dental X-rays or reports, a list of medicines you take, and details of any medical conditions or allergies.

Are you open on Saturdays?

Yes. We're open every day of the week, including Saturday, from 8 AM to 7 PM.

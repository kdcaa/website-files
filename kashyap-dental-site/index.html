Services

Treatment, start to finish.

General Dentistry

Check-ups, cleanings, fillings, and everyday dental care.

The foundation of a healthy mouth: finding small problems early, before they become painful or expensive.

  • Dental examination and advice
  • Scaling and polishing (cleaning)
  • Tooth-coloured fillings
  • Dental X-rays when needed

Most people benefit from a check-up about every six months; your dentist will suggest what suits you.

Cosmetic & Aesthetics

Whitening, smile design, and aesthetic dental treatments.

Treatments that improve how your smile looks, planned around what you want to change.

  • Teeth whitening
  • Smile design consultation
  • Aesthetic reshaping and restorations

Every cosmetic plan starts with a check-up, so the teeth and gums are healthy before treatment begins.

Orthodontics

Braces and aligner-based treatment for all ages.

Straightening teeth and correcting the bite, for children, teenagers and adults.

  • Assessment of teeth and bite
  • Fixed braces
  • Clear aligners
  • Retainers after treatment

Treatment length depends on the case, typically months to a couple of years. You'll get a clear plan before starting.

Oral Surgery

Extractions and minor surgical procedures, done with care.

Procedures carried out under local anaesthesia, with time taken to explain each step.

  • Tooth extractions
  • Minor oral surgical procedures
  • Written aftercare instructions

Tell us about any medicines you take (especially blood thinners) before your appointment.

Root Canal Treatment

Pain relief and tooth preservation with modern technique.

Saves a badly decayed or infected tooth instead of removing it.

  • Infected pulp is removed under local anaesthesia
  • The canals are cleaned, shaped and sealed
  • A crown is often advised afterwards to protect the tooth

Usually completed in one to three visits. Most people find it feels similar to having a filling.

Pediatric Dentistry

Gentle dental care designed for children.

Friendly, patient care that helps children feel comfortable at the dentist.

  • First dental visits and check-ups
  • Fluoride application and fissure sealants
  • Fillings for milk and permanent teeth
  • Brushing and diet advice for parents

A first visit is recommended when the first tooth appears, or by the child's first birthday.

Ask a question
How often should I get a dental check-up?

For most people, about every six months. Some need visits more or less often depending on their gums, cavities and general health; your dentist will advise.

Is root canal treatment painful?

It's done under local anaesthesia, so most people feel little during the procedure; many say it feels much like getting a filling. Some tenderness for a few days afterwards is normal.

When should my child first see a dentist?

When the first tooth comes in, or by the first birthday. Early visits catch problems early and help children get used to the dentist.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Any previous dental X-rays or reports, a list of medicines you take, and details of any medical conditions or allergies.
